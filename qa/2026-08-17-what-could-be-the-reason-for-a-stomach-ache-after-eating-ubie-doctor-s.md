Explanation

Stomach aches after eating can happen for different reasons. Sometimes, the body has a strong reaction to certain foods, which can cause pain in the stomach. This is explained by new research that looks at how food affects the stomach and intestines. Other times, a condition called gastroparesis might be the cause. Gastroparesis is when the stomach takes too long to empty its contents, leading to discomfort and pain after meals.

Here are some possible reasons for stomach aches after eating:

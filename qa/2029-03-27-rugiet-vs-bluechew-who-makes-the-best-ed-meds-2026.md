Rugiet Ready vs BlueChew Gold

Advantage: BlueChew Gold

Rugiet and BlueChew’s respective flagship products are decidedly similar. Both offer a combination of sildenafil, tadalafil, and apomorphine to treat ED. But between the two, BlueChew Gold has a slight dosing advantage, as well as a fourth active ingredient that can tackle ED from another angle altogether. To better understand how these multitherapies function, let’s quickly break down their key ingredients:

Sildenafil

Sildenafil is a PDE5 inhibitor and the generic active ingredient in Viagra. It reduces the breakdown of cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), a critical player in erectile blood flow.7 8 With healthy cGMP levels, men with organic, physiological ED can have their normal erectile function restored, but only for as long as sildenafil is active, which is about 6-8 hours after the 30-60 minutes it takes to kick in.9 Therefore, sildenafil is typically taken as needed.

BlueChew and Rugiet both include sildenafil in Gold and Ready, but at their comparable dose levels, BlueChew provides either as much or a little more sildenafil than Rugiet. BlueChew also offers sildenafil in simpler multitherapies and as a stand-alone chewable monotherapy for men who aren’t candidates for Gold.

Tadalfil

Tadalafil is also a PDE5 inhibitor, but it lasts longer than sildenafil — up to 36 hours per dose.10 It’s also available in slightly lower doses as a daily medication, allowing you to be ready for arousal at any time. Like sildenafil, it also takes 30-60 minutes to kick in when taken as needed.

Tadalafil forms the basis of every Rugiet medication that aims to fight ED, even those with additional targets such as hair loss or premature ejaculation. BlueChew also uses it in a handful of treatments and as a stand-alone monotherapy for men who can’t safely take Gold.

Apomorphine

Apomorphine works on dopamine receptors in the brain — a far cry from the physiological activities of a PDE5 inhibitor.11 But brain chemistry is a critical part of erectile health, with disruptions often being referred to as neurogenic ED.12 It has a good amount of positive research behind it in erectile health, but it never caught on the way PDE5 inhibitors did.13

Both BlueChew and Rugiet include apomorphine only in their flagship products — nowhere else in their catalogs and never as a monotherapy. Research indicates that 3mg might be a sweet spot, but BlueChew offers one option in its dose range as high as 4mg for men with higher BMIs or metabolisms that simply require that little extra.

Oxytocin

Researchers often refer to oxytocin as “the love hormone” due to the role it plays in social and emotional connection.14 It’s also ingrained in libido and mental health, leading studies to examine its potential to improve either. In both sexes, oxytocin has been shown to improve mental health markers of depression and anxiety, and it’s additionally been shown to improve libido in women, though not so much in men.15 16

So, for men with ED, oxytocin may not offer much of a libido boost, but it could help the roughly 40% of ED cases that are psychogenic in nature — arising from mental health challenges like those oxytocin has been shown to improve.6 BlueChew includes oxytocin at three dose levels in its Gold multitherapy, though most research uses intranasal administration, which complicates any effort to find an appropriate oral dose. Rugiet does not employ it anywhere.

Its ED/hair loss combination, for example, provides 6mg of daily tadalafil alongside 3mg of minoxidil. Ultimately, if Rugiet could improve the dose flexibility issue and offer a few simpler ED treatments, it could run away with this category. Who’s safer, Rugiet or BlueChew?

Between BlueChew Gold and Rugiet Ready, Rugiet has what could be the safer product. That’s because it offers a lower-dose introductory level than BlueChew does. That allows men to start at a theoretically safer place to see whether combining these drugs could incite certain adverse effects. If efficacy is merely fine, and there are no side effects, then you could titrate up to a dose that’s more comparable between the two companies.

Rugiet also gets some points here because it excludes oxytocin from its formula. Not that oxytocin is particularly dangerous — research indicates it should be safe for most people19 — but any added active ingredient increases side effect risks, so eliding oxytocin from its formula gives Rugiet a small edge for safety.

Beyond that, the companies are more or less tied. They both carry out thorough medical intakes that lead to legal, legitimate prescriptions provided by licensed medical professionals. The pharmacies they use are FDA-regulated and deliver LegalScript-certified medications. So, what wins this category for Rugiet turns out to be its medication’s simplicity and flexibility.

Does Rugiet or BlueChew taste better?

To date, the only chewable ED medication that’s actually tasted good to our testers has been Roman’s Daily Rise Gummies, a daily tadalafil chewable. The problem that arises with most other chewable ED meds is that the taste of the medicine is too strong to mask. With BlueChew Gold and Rugiet Ready, we encounter two very different flavor profiles. Alternatives to Rugiet and BlueChew

Whether the prospect of lozenges and chewable medications doesn’t interest you enough for you to justify spending more money on the same therapies, or you’re just curious to see whether other companies offer similar novel formulas for less, there’s a growing landscape of telemedical outfits offering products in competition with those you can get from Rugiet and BlueChew.

Here, we examine a handful of the top players in this space to reveal the specific ways they might make superior choices for some men seeking treatments akin to those in Rugiet’s and BlueChew’s catalogs.

Hims

Hims offers several multitherapies, some of which are available in chewable form. In some cases, it offers better deals than BlueChew or Rugiet, but in others, it falls short. For example, you can purchase its 11.2mg as-needed tadalafil in a chewable form on a five-month billing cycle that brings the cost per dose down to just $1.30, whereas neither BlueChew nor Rugiet offer standalone tadalafil in any format, and competitors to Hims don’t come close to this low of a price. However, Hims’ as-needed multitherapy combining 45mg of sildenafil with 11.2mg of tadalafil can only come down to $6 per dose. Again, neither BlueChew nor Rugiet offer sildenafil on its own, but competitors to Hims, like Rocket Rx, have much better sildenafil prices.

It’s also worth noting that Hims doesn’t offer any ED multitherapy containing either apomorphine or oxytocin, so Rugiet and BlueChew remain superior options for men seeking the most comprehensive single treatments they can find.

Roman

We previously mentioned Roman’s Daily Rise Gummies as one of the few chewable ED medications that actually taste good. The flip side to that positive aspect is price, an issue that plagues much of Roman’s catalog. The closest thing Roman has to Rugiet’s or BlueChew’s offerings is its Ro Sparks multitherapy combining 55mg of sildenafil with 22mg of tadalafil in a sublingual lozenge.

But the lowest cost for Ro Sparks is $12/dose, a massive increase compared to Rocket Rx and even Hims’ alternatives.

Rocket Rx

Rocket Rx has a catalog that compares well with Hims, but it also delivers some very specific dose combinations in its multitherapies combining sildenafil and tadalafil. The most exciting of these is actually its weakest, with just 20mg of sildenafil alongside 5mg of tadalafil. The reason for the excitement is that most multitherapies start their dosing much higher, immediately excluding men interested in multitherapies but who may not have tried any ED medications to that point.

Far better for these men to start with a lower dose where they can and only titrate upward as needed. That can ensure they’re getting an effective dose while remaining as safe as possible. And with per-dose prices as low as $1.64, Rocket’s offerings are also the lowest-cost multis on the market.

House Calls for Urgent Injury Treatment

Urgent injuries can happen when you least expect them. When your primary care physician isn’t available and urgent care is not an option, a house call doctor can provide prompt assistance with many types of urgent injuries, all in the comfort of your home or hotel.

What Are Some Common Urgent Injuries We Treat?

Concierge MD treats many kinds of urgent injuries in the comfort of your home. We bring your appointment directly to you, saving you the hassle of transit to a clinic or hours in a waiting room just to see a doctor. A doctor’s appointment at home also minimizes your exposure to other patients at your local clinic who may be ill, reducing your chance of catching another illness while you are waiting to be seen for your injury.

# Roman Reigns Is Playing The Rock's Brother in 'Hobbs & Shaw'

[Is Playing The Rock's](https://allpepay.com/blog/8775244512.html)

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson won't be the only enormous Samoan flexing in his upcoming Fast & Furious spinoff Hobbs & Shaw. The movie star took to Instagram to announce that he'll be accompanied in the movie, due to be released August 2, by none other than Joe Anoaʻi, otherwise known as Roman Reigns.

Roman Reigns is part of the famed Anoaʻi wrestling family and is signed to WWE, performing for its Raw, though he's currently on hiatus due to a battle with leukemia. He's also a former professional gridiron football player. And now, he gets to be The Rock's brother. Johnson's touted the "exclusive" news that Roman Reigns will play his sibling in Hobbs & Shaw, firmly planting himself in the ever-expanding Fast & Furious universe.

"The brothers roar," Johnson captioned the photo of him and Roman Reigns filming on the set of Hobbs & Shaw, both shirtless and looking rather intimidating. "Good times droppin’ sweat and spillin’ blood with my family, @RomanReigns."

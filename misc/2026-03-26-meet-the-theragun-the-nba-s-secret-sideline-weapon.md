You may not be able to drain three-pointers and dissect the Golden State Warriors the way Kyrie Irving does, but you just may be able to borrow his secret weapon against injuries.

Because the Cleveland Cavaliers point guard apparently has some help in staying healthy. During all the hubbub over the Warriors trouncing Irving’s Cavs for the NBA title, you may have missed a brief Monday night shot of Irving on the sidelines, a trainer huddled over him with a blue-and-black gun.

That gun is called a Theragun and while it's pricey at $600, it's also an emerging tool for athletes looking to get a quick fascia fix on game days.

The Theragun looks more like a power tool than your average recovery device, but it's a powerful tool in the right hands. Essentially, it's aiming to do the same thing you try to do when you foam roll, loosening muscles and relaxing tendons. We brought a Theragun into the Men's Health Manhattan offices to try it out on ourselves, and the device certainly got raves. Most people enjoyed it - some, even a little too much.

For Irving, it seemed to work throughout the NBA Finals - and even this season. The guard played in a career-high 72 games this year, and he was at his best over the last three games of the Finals, dropping 34.7 points a night in a losing Cavs cause.

The Theragun works just as easily for you, too. We at Men's Health's Manhattan offices have put the device through its paces over the last few weeks. It draws quick stares because of its intimidating exterior (nobody really wants to get a "massage" from a construction tool), but just as quickly, those stares turn to smiles when people feel the surprisingly gentle pulse of the Theragun along their backs and shoulders.

# Olympian Cody Miller Showed Off the High-Tech Backyard Pool He Uses to Train in Quarantine

[Cody Miller Showed](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/7409515932.html)

The postponement of the 2020 Summer Games has left this year's Olympic hopefuls with plenty of extra time on their hands, which they are dedicating to preparations for next year. However, as Team USA swimmer (and double Olympic medalist) Cody Miller explained in a recent YouTube video, accessing the necessary training facilities has been all-but impossible during the pandemic.

"With the current state of the world, with COVID and lockdown, finding a pool to swim at regularly, even for Olympic swimmers like myself, has been very difficult," he said. "Sometimes I'm driving up to three hours a day just to make sure I get in a pool and swim. Other times I go weeks at a time when there are no pools in the nearby area to swim in."

That changed a little over a month ago, however, when after a series of home improvement projects, he made the newest addition to his yard: a Cody Miller-branded outdoor swimming pool from Endless Pools Elite. The remote-controlled stroke training system can be programmed to enable an athlete to practice swimming against a variety of currents at different paces, and Miller has been using it to catch up on his training for Tokyo 2021.

"I've been training in this thing for the past month and it can crank," he said. "You can't outswim this thing. You can do virtually anything; race pace training, a slow aerobic set, a painful distance threshold set, some 200 pace... Trust me, I've been doing it."

The entire unit took about five days to assemble and install, and just like all his other quarantine DIY, Miller put it together himself (with a little help from a trained specialist).

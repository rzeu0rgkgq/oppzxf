What is Aspergillosis?

Aspergillosis is an infection caused by a type of fungus. It typically affects the respiratory system and sinuses, but its signs and severity vary greatly. Aspergillus is present everywhere (indoors and outdoors), which can cause the infection.

Diagnostic Questions for Aspergillosis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Does your cough worsen after exercise?

Do you have yellow or green sputum?

Are you producing phlegm that contains blood?

Do you experience wheezing or whistling sounds when breathing?

Have you ever been diagnosed with a connective tissue, autoimmune, or rheumatic disease?

Treatment of Aspergillosis

Treatment for aspergillosis mainly involves antifungal drugs, which may be taken orally or injected.

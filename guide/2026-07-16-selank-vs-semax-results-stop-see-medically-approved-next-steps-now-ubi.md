1. What Are Selank and Semax?

Selank

Synthetic analog of the immune peptide tuftsin.

Developed to reduce anxiety and boost mood without sedation.

Administered as a short nasal spray regimen.

Semax

Synthetic fragment of adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH).

Designed to enhance attention, memory, and neuroprotection.

Also given intranasally in short cycles.

Both peptides cross the blood-brain barrier when delivered nasally. They act on neurotransmitter systems (e.g., GABA, serotonin, dopamine), neurotrophins (e.g., BDNF), and stress hormones to deliver their effects.

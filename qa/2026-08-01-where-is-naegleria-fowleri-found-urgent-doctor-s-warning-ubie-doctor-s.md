Naegleria fowleri, often called the "brain-eating amoeba," is a microscopic organism found in warm freshwater environments like lakes, rivers, and hot springs. Infection occurs when contaminated water enters the nose, allowing the amoeba to travel to the brain. While infections are extremely rare, they are serious and require prompt medical attention. Explanation

Naegleria fowleri is a type of amoeba that lives in warm freshwater environments. It is often found in places like lakes, rivers, and hot springs, especially during the summer months when the water is warm. This amoeba can cause a rare but severe brain infection if it enters the body through the nose.

# Dress for the Weather

I'm not a big umbrella fan; I think they get in the way. As long as you're dressed properly, you should be okay. My kids have never met a puddle they didn't like, because kids know that rain is a special thing. Not adults. We try to get out of it immediately. But it's rain, not sulfuric acid. Prepare properly, and it'll bring out the kid in you, too.

The secret is to do a lot of layering. For the top layer, I wear a 15-year-old rain slicker from Barbour that I get oiled about once a year. You feel like you're wearing a substantial piece of clothing. It has some tradition behind it. If I'm going into a mess, that's what I want keeping me from the elements.

The right boots make all the difference. I have a pair of rain boots in classic yellow -- they're great for sloshing about on the farm. And if I'm on air, I'll wear a pair of paraboots. They're sturdy and keep water out, but they look good.

All storms have their moments. Sometimes you just have to wait them out. I remember covering one at Kitty Hawk, in the surf. There was sand blowing...it was like we were undergoing dermabrasion. Our faces had a dewy glow afterward. When you're in the middle of bad weather, you don't really think about it because of your fear and the adrenaline that's pumping through your veins.

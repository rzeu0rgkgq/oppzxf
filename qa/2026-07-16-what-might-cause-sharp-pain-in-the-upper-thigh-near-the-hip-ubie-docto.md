Explanation

Sharp pain in the upper thigh near the hip can have several causes. One common cause is greater trochanter bursitis, which is more often seen in females. This condition involves inflammation of the bursa, a small fluid-filled sac near the hip joint, leading to pain in the thigh area.

Another potential cause could be thigh pain following hip surgery, such as after total hip arthroplasty. This type of surgery is performed to replace a hip joint, and some patients may experience thigh pain afterward.

What is Chyluria?

Chyluria is a rare condition where lymphatic fluid leaks into the kidneys, making the urine milky white. It's caused by lymph flow obstruction, which can be due to filariasis (a parasitic disease affecting lymph nodes and vessels), trauma, surgery, or tumors.

Typical Symptoms of Chyluria

Diagnostic Questions for Chyluria

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Is your urine exhibiting a milky-white color?

Is your urine cloudy?

Do you have a fever?

Have you noticed any lumps on your skin?

Treatment of Chyluria

Treatment involves addressing the underlying cause along with medications to reduce chyle production, a low-fat diet and IV (intravenous) procedures to repair the lymph vessel.

What should I do if I experience tingling on one side of my face?

Reviewed by Tomas Diaz, MD

Emergency Medicine

If you notice tingling on one side of your face, especially if it starts suddenly or is accompanied by other symptoms, you should seek medical attention to rule out serious causes.

Explanation

Tingling on one side of the face can be caused by various issues, ranging from mild nerve irritation to more serious conditions like stroke or nerve disorders. If the tingling begins suddenly, is severe, or comes with other symptoms such as weakness, numbness in other body parts, difficulty speaking, vision changes, or severe headache, it’s important to get emergency medical care immediately.

If the tingling develops gradually, is mild, and doesn’t have other concerning symptoms, you should still see a healthcare provider to identify the cause and get appropriate treatment.
